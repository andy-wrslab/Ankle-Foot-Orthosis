Bottom-box cap

A shroud that slides straight back over the box. A flange rides over the heatsink shelf and hooks the two vertical screws; the cables leave through three notches that a clamp bar closes into holes. Every dimension is estimated from the photo, including the mock of the box it sits on.

How it goes on

  1. Back the two nuts off the vertical screws a few turns.
  2. Slide the cap straight back over the box. The flange runs over the shelf and the screws enter its rear-open slots; the cheeks keep it square.
  3. Tighten the nuts from above.
  4. Lay the cables into the three notches, then fit the clamp bar with its two M3 screws. Each notch becomes a closed hole that pinches the bundle.
